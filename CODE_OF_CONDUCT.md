# Code of Conduct

## Purpose
This code of conduct defines expected behavior for contributors and community members to foster an open, welcoming, and productive environment for all who participate in this project.

## Scope
Applies to all project spaces including code repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, project wikis, mailing lists, chat rooms, and events organized by the project.

## Expected behavior
- Be welcoming, respectful, and courteous.
- Assume good faith and seek to resolve misunderstandings constructively.
- Use inclusive language and avoid dismissive or exclusionary comments.
- Give credit and be mindful of others’ time and effort.
- Follow repository contribution guidelines and use clear, polite communication.

## Unacceptable behavior
Examples include, but are not limited to:
- Harassment, threats, or intimidation in any form.
- Derogatory comments or slurs related to identity (race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, religion, etc.).
- Personal attacks, insults, or public shaming.
- Publishing private information (doxing) or unwanted sexual attention.
- Sustained disruption of community processes, trolling, or spamming.

## Reporting
If you experience or observe behavior that violates this code of conduct:
1. Document the incident (links, screenshots, timestamps).
2. Report it to the project maintainers at rabanser@princeton.edu.
3. If the incident involves immediate danger, contact local authorities.

Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible.

## Enforcement
Maintainers will review reports and may take actions including:
- Private warning to the offending party.
- Requirement to apologize, follow additional guidelines, or attend mediation.
- Temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
- Removal of offending content or contributions.

Decisions and corrective steps will be communicated to the reporter and the person involved. Appeals can be submitted to the maintainer team at the same contact address.

## Attribution and updates
This code of conduct may be updated by the maintainers. Changes will be announced in repository communication channels. Contributors are encouraged to raise concerns or suggestions about the policy by contacting the maintainers.
